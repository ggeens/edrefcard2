📖 Mapper guide

How to add a new controller to EDRefCard with the mapping editor. A typical stick takes 10-20 minutes.

What you are building

A mapping ties a clean photo of a controller to its physical controls: each box groups the rows of one physical control (a hat, a trigger, an axis...) and points to it with a leader line. When a player uploads their .binds file, the engine fills your boxes with their bindings and hides the unused ones. So the mapping should cover every control on the device, not just the ones you personally bind.

Workflow

  1. Open the Controllers page. If the device is in the Unknown devices queue, click Scaffold: the editor opens pre-filled from a real .binds. Otherwise open the Mapping Editor directly.
  2. Upload a clean product photo (manufacturer press shot or your own, no overlays, no watermarks). Keep "side margins" ticked: the photo is placed in the middle of a wider canvas so your boxes have room on both sides.
  3. No .binds to start from? Use Blank scaffold (buttons / POVs / axes counts) to generate the rows from the manual.
  4. Use the stamps for anything missing: pick a stamp, then click the physical control on the photo. One click per control; a 4-way hat with push is a single click, its 5 rows are pre-filled and Joy_N numbering auto-increments.
  5. For scaffolded boxes, click Set btn then click the control on the photo to anchor the leader line.
  6. Click Arrange: boxes are sorted into two columns by anchor position, so leader lines never cross. Sizes are normalised automatically. Do not spend time pixel-pushing box sizes.
  7. Fix labels, numbers and Joy_N values in the right-hand panel (check them against the manual or a .binds).
  8. Preview renders the real card server-side. Iterate until the layout reads well.
  9. Save draft. Every save, including an admin's save, is a draft. If this controller is already published, its public revision stays unchanged. An admin reviews the saved draft and uses Publish draft on Controllers to release it.

Stamps

StampOne click gives you
Button1 row (press), Joy_N auto-incremented
Hat 4-way4 rows (up/right/down/left), Joy_N..N+3
Hat 4-way + push5 rows, Joy_N..N+4
POV4 rows Joy_POVnUp/Right/Down/Left
2-stage triggerstage 1 + stage 2 rows
Stick (X+Y)2 analogue rows (Joy_XAxis / Joy_YAxis)
Axis1 empty analogue row (set the Joy_?Axis yourself)

Axis names in Elite: X/Y = stick, Z = twist, RZ = brake/rudder, U = slider, V = dial. POV hats send Joy_POVn*, thumb hats usually send plain Joy_N buttons; a real .binds settles any doubt.

Shortcuts & safety nets

  • Ctrl + mouse wheel zoom (around the cursor) · Space + drag pan · Fit resets
  • Arrows nudge the selected box (Shift = x10) · Del delete · Ctrl+D duplicate
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo/redo (100 full-document steps, including imports)
  • Your unsaved work is stored in this browser. After a crash or refresh, choose Restore local draft or Discard local draft before editing. Downloads and server saves are still recommended; browser storage can be unavailable.
  • If someone else saved the same mapping while you were editing, Save is refused with a message instead of overwriting their work

Ground rules

  • Exhaustive beats personal: map every physical control, not just your own bindings. Players with different profiles must be able to use the card.
  • Cite your source: manufacturer manual or callout sheet preferred; your own hardware is even better.
  • Photos: neutral background, straight-on angle, high resolution. Ask if unsure about rights.
  • L/R variants: map one side only; an admin can generate the mirrored variant in one click.
  • Stuck, or want a review before publish? Ping the admin (forum DM or Discord). Saving a draft never breaks anything public.
